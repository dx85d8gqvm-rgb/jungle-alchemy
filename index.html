Good Questions

The things you’re probably wondering.

Anything else, ask on the call.

Why Costa Rica? +

Because it’s the opposite of a casino in every way that matters. Rainforest and Pacific instead of carpet and fluorescent light. Warm water waves. Organic food grown down the road. A slow pace, a safe and welcoming country, and, in January, the exact opposite climate to Las Vegas or wherever winter has you. It’s the best place I’ve found for a reset.

Do I need yoga or meditation experience? +

No. Most people who come have little or none. Everything is taught from the ground up and adapted to whoever is in the room. If you can sit in a chair and breathe, you have all the prerequisites.

Is this going to be woo-woo? +

No. This is a grounded event focused on you having an incredible time.

What does a day look like? +

Every day is a little different, but this is the shape of it. We’ll start most days with a mix of yoga, breathwork, and meditation, then breakfast. Late morning is the group excursion: waterfall adventures, group hike, or the farmer’s market. Lunch, then time to relax in the creek. Afternoons bring sound journeys or surfing at sunset at the beach, followed by dinner and an evening activity. Nothing is packed too tight.

Will there be alcohol? +

No. There won’t be alcohol, but there will be fresh coconut water and organic fruit smoothies.

Will there be poker strategy discussions? +

No, there will not be intentional time set to discuss poker strategy. This event is designed for you to take a break from the grind, although guests are welcome to discuss strategy with each other if they feel called. Guests are also welcome to discuss strategy with Beau during the downtime.

Who else is coming? +

Poker players and entrepreneurs, mostly in their late twenties to forties, who are doing well on paper and want to feel as good as their results look. The group is intentionally small and capped, and every person is accepted by application, so the room is people you’ll actually want to know.

Can I come alone? +

Yes, it’s encouraged! Couples and friends are also welcome, and can room together.

Can I get a private room? +

Yes. Rooms range from private eco-luxury suites with en-suite bathrooms to comfortable shared spaces, all air-conditioned. We’ll help you pick on the call. See all the rooms.

How do I get there? +

Fly into San José (SJO). Direct flights run from most major US cities. Everything is handled from there. We’ll have a private shuttle take you to the retreat center.

Is there wifi? Can I still work or play? +

Yes, there’s wifi. And you’ll have plenty of downtime to chill in a hammock, enjoy the creek on the property, or be online.

What happens after I apply? +

The application takes a few minutes and leads straight to booking a call with Beau. On the call you’ll walk through the fit, the rooms, the details, and the investment. If it’s a yes on both sides, you’ll get everything you need to lock in your spot and start planning January.
