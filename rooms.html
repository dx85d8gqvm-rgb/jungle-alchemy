Where You’ll Stay

Nine ways to call it home.

From the two-story Luna Master Suite to easygoing semi-dorms, every room is wrapped in jungle. Take a look, and we’ll help you find your fit on the call.

No. 06 · Sacred Geometry

Flower of Life Room

Stacked Beds · Private Bathroom · Shared Patio

The Flower of Life is a comfortable and stylish room with a private bathroom. It contains two stacked beds with extra-thick, deluxe pillowtop mattresses, a large closet with shelves and drawers, and a couch seating area. It shares a patio with the adjacent Hamsa room, a secluded nook to relax and enjoy the sights and sounds of the surrounding nature.

  • ♠2 stacked beds with pillowtop mattresses
  • ♥Private bathroom
  • ♦Large closet with shelves & drawers
  • ♣Couch seating area
  • ♠Shared patio with Hamsa room

No. 07 · Protection & Warmth

Hamsa Room

Stacked Beds · Private Bathroom · Shared Patio

The Hamsa room is stylishly decorated with modern and eastern motifs. There are 2 stacked beds with extra-thick, deluxe pillowtop mattresses, a large wooden storage for your things, and a private bathroom. There are comfortable places to relax inside and out, whether on the sofa or on the patio shared with the Flower of Life room.

  • ♠2 stacked beds with pillowtop mattresses
  • ♥Private bathroom
  • ♦Large wooden storage
  • ♣Sofa seating area
  • ♠Shared patio with Flower of Life room

No. 08 · Under the Stars

Estrella Room

2 Single Beds · Private Bathroom · Private Space

Situated on the ground floor of the Master Suite, the Estrella Room has 2 single beds with extra-thick, deluxe pillowtop mattresses, a private bathroom, and a large closet. It shares the Master Suite’s large downstairs lounging areas and kitchen.

  • ♠2 single beds with pillowtop mattresses
  • ♥Private bathroom
  • ♦Large closet
  • ♣Shared downstairs lounge & kitchen
  • ♠Ground floor of Master Suite

No. 09 · Community & Connection

Colibri Semi-Dorm Rooms

Shared Beds · Shared Bathroom · Social Vibe

On the ground floor of the expansive elevated yoga deck are 2 “semi-dorm” rooms, each with 2 full-sized beds. We call them “semi-dorm” since, though they each have their own doors for privacy, they share the same central area and bathroom.

  • ♠2 full-sized beds per room
  • ♥Private door per room
  • ♦Shared central area & bathroom
  • ♣Below the elevated yoga deck
